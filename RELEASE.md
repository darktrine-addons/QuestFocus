## v0.9.5-beta — 2026-11-22

### New features

- feat: **QuestFocus now runs on WoW Forever.** The same download works on Retail and Forever; the CurseForge and Wago apps will offer it for both. Zone filtering and the party progress dots work as on Retail. Modes built around modern content (campaign, weeklies, Important) will mostly come up empty in Forever, simply because those quest types are rare there.
